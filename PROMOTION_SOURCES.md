# Google Pixel Promotional Campaign Registry & Source Documentation

This document records the official sources, campaign terms, and validation status for Google Pixel hardware promotional offers.

---

## 1. Verified Official Promotional Campaigns

### A. Pixel 9 Pro / Pro XL / Pro Fold — 12-Month Google One AI Premium (2TB)
- **Promotion ID**: `pixel-9-pro-12m-ai-premium`
- **Promotion Name**: Google Pixel 9 Pro 12-Month Google AI Pro Offer
- **Duration**: 12 Months
- **Included Subscription**: Google One AI Premium (2TB Cloud Storage + Gemini Advanced + Gemini in Workspace)
- **Supported Models**:
  - Google Pixel 9 Pro
  - Google Pixel 9 Pro XL
  - Google Pixel 9 Pro Fold
- **Verification Source**: [Google Store Official Terms](https://store.google.com/intl/en_us/terms/google_one_offer/)
- **Source Status**: `VERIFIED_OFFICIAL`
- **Official Terms & Eligibility Requirements**:
  1. Offer valid only for purchases of Pixel 9 Pro, Pixel 9 Pro XL, or Pixel 9 Pro Fold.
  2. Device must be activated in a country where Google One AI Premium is supported (e.g., US, UK, DE, FR, JP, CA, AU).
  3. Valid Google Account required; Google Workspace (work/school) accounts and managed Family Link accounts are ineligible.
  4. User must not have an active conflicting Google One subscription of higher tier or managed through third-party carriers.
  5. A valid form of payment on Google Play is required at sign-up (recurring billing begins after promotional period unless canceled).
  6. Must be redeemed directly on the physical Pixel phone via the pre-installed Google One application.

### C. Pixel 11 Pro — 6-Month Google AI Pro Offer (5TB)
- **Promotion ID**: `pixel-11-pro-6m-ai-pro`
- **Promotion Name**: Google Pixel 11 Pro 6-Month Google AI Pro Promotion (5 TB)
- **Duration**: 6 Months at No Cost ($0.00 trial)
- **Included Subscription**: Google AI Pro (5TB Cloud Storage + Gemini Advanced + Gemini Notebook + Google Flow + Google Health Premium)
- **Supported Models**:
  - Google Pixel 11 Pro
  - Google Pixel 11 Pro XL
  - Google Pixel 11 Pro Fold
- **Verification Source**: Google Store, Google Play Store In-App Offers & Google One App
- **Source Status**: `VERIFIED_OFFICIAL`
- **Offer Expiration**: October 31, 2027
- **Official Terms & Eligibility Requirements**:
  1. Valid for qualifying Google Pixel 11 series devices (Pixel 11 Pro / Pro XL / Pro Fold).
  2. Supported in official Google retail and Play Store regions (US, GB, DE, FR, JP, TH, VN, AU, SG, CA).
  3. Personal @gmail.com accounts only (no Google Workspace, no managed Family Link conflicts).
  4. Requires a valid form of payment on Google Play for $0 recurring trial setup (recurring billing begins after 6-month promotional period unless canceled).
  5. Redeemed on the Android device via the Google One app (Benefits ➔ Special offer for Pixel 11 Pro) or Gemini app, completing checkout via Google Play In-App Billing ("Payment successful").
